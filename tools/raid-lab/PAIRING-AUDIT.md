## Limits

- Unsupported kits are explicitly excluded; imported pairings do not make those kits executable.
- Static enabler matches must still pass actual trigger, targeting, rotation and uptime checks in simulation.
- Whole team examples require every named member and the specified role slots. Missing members no longer produce a partial-team bonus.
- Search tests partner packages jointly and keeps only simulated improvements. It is bounded by the selected time budget.
- Source review captures were unavailable for a fresh full-text audit. This audit uses the stored relationship facts and resolved local skill definitions.
- Named event semantics, incoming damage, geometry and unsupported skill mechanics remain subject to the combat engine limitations.

## Executed validation

- 129 distinct supported kits each completed a 30-second simulation.
- 10 paired/unpaired 180-second simulations checked Blanc, Bready, Arcana, Crown and Prika. All expected activation/presence checks passed.
- CPU and GPU eligibility checks accept damage dealers of any class and Burst stage.
- A complete five-team search passed ownership, non-overlap, full-duration and elemental-damage requirements.
- Twelve rotation regression tests cover long B1/B2 cooldowns, activated personal/favorite-item CDR, alternating supports, Isabel, Modernia, and Bunny Soda's stack thresholds and off-burst duration changes.
- All five saved current-roster raid teams passed the new rotation safeguard with unchanged damage. A separate 60-simulation constrained search selected Arcana and Isabel with Crown as a second B2.
- The app reports measured Full Burst windows, uptime and support-stage delays; duration effects are included in whole-fight damage comparisons.
