Model notes & credits

Combat calculation

Raid Lab uses the MIT-licensed Jgaram/nikke-calc engine as a separate dependency, with local support for unequipped cubes and planned squad cover. It advances combat in 1/60-second steps, processing weapon attacks, skill triggers, buffs, reloads, accumulated burst gauge and burst priorities. The app does not open or control NIKKE.

Expected critical and core-hit damage and a fixed seed are used. The engine's static boss model does not establish survival, campaign clear success, interception success, finite part health, target movement or phase timing. Skill-kit availability indicates implemented data, not independent accuracy certification.

Search

A build-aware score proposes diverse, non-overlapping sets of teams. It includes parsed HP-to-ATK conversions and bounded skill coefficients without weighting a unit by class. Standard B1/B2/B3/B3/flex candidates receive a 30-second screening simulation; the top two plans receive full-duration simulation. Rotation alternatives receive 60-second screens and full-fight verification even when CDR is not required. Cooldown, gauge and duration options can earn a comparison; only higher simulated damage wins. Exchanges with other squads use their combined damage. Search results are recommendations, not a global optimum. Special burst chains can be explored through the manual simulator.

Local synergy weights and burst preferences help prioritize candidates and owned alternatives. A build-only starting candidate and alternative burst checks remain available. Guide material was consulted during development; no articles are bundled and no guide website is contacted during search. Preferences are not damage multipliers or a guarantee of the best team.

Automatic fight reports separate direct boss damage, part-destruction HP loss and summon damage. Character Battle Records include raw summon hits and overkill; the boss comparison excludes those hits. Part destruction changes boss HP and damage-driven phases without adding to character damage or lifesteal. Museum leaderboard accounting is not yet verified.

Roster data

English names and name-code mapping come from the public BlaBlaLink catalogue. Exia character-data JSON is read through an independently written adapter; no Exia code is included. Complete BlaBlaLink account exports preserve equipment, cube, bond, collection and research as well as skills and limit breaks. Exia exports preserve the fields present in their format. Missing build details remain explicit assumptions. BlaBlaLink sign-in happens in a dedicated browser profile saved on this PC. Account reads run inside that browser, which keeps its session cookies; passwords and session cookies are never included in roster exports. Rosters are saved in this browser and the app’s local private folder.

Modelled auto-play uses a 0.5-second stage-switch delay, which is a stated approximation. Assisted play allows one burst-gauge tap shooter and uses a 0.1-second switch delay. Gear/cube allocations are taken as supplied; the search does not allocate a limited inventory of cubes between squads.

Boss presets and cover

Presets include Solo Raids 39–40 and Museum Halls 1–9. Automatic boss models track finite parts, summons, barriers and timed cover. Finite part HP uses the recovered client constructor and the selected variant’s part-HP table; main-body HP is separate. Mother Whale’s source skill slots distinguish Challenge and No Limit summons. Summon control records whether the squad cleared adds before protection casts, independently of the core deadline. Complete score, geometry, timing, survival and Museum buffs still require gameplay calibration. No automatic current-raid refresh is provided.

Sources checked 10 September 2026: Raid 40 data, Raid 39 data, Museum reference. Internal profiles retain known attack descriptions and unresolved mechanics.

Content rules and unfinished mechanics

Special Interception uses level 200; Anomaly caps imported levels at 400. Campaign recommendations use area damage and team utility; an entered CP deficit reduces effective stats using the published CP penalty table. No campaign clear or stage DPS is predicted.

Automatic Kraken now executes a recovered boss script with finite tentacles, interruption checks, attacks, cover and healing. Its physical calibration remains incomplete, and it must not be treated as a verified clear predictor. Other bosses retain partial models. Target HP, exact timing, aiming rules, finite parts and survival need verification. Team results therefore do not certify a clear. Optional immunity windows are user assumptions. Sources: Interception rules, Anomaly rules.
